## Context

An enterprise fiber network platform used by many tenant companies. Tenants and their managers needed to see the state of their business at a glance: client invoices, connections, paid revenue and other financial and operational metrics. Each company, and each person in it, cared about a different set of numbers.

## What I did

- Built user-customizable drag-and-drop dashboards on GridStack.
- Shipped 10+ widget types covering client invoices, connections, paid revenue and other business metrics, charted with ApexCharts.
- Synced each user's layout to the backend.

## Result

Each user arranges their own dashboard from a shared set of widgets, and the layout follows them across sessions.
